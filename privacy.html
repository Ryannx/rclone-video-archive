Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

"rclone video archive" is a personal backup configuration used only by its owner. It runs rclone locally on the owner's computer.

What it accesses

It requests the Google Drive drive.file scope, which allows it to create files and read only the files it created. It cannot see any other files in Google Drive.

What it collects

Nothing. No data is sent to any server other than Google Drive itself. Files are transferred directly between the owner's computer and the owner's own Google Drive. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party sharing.

Storage of credentials

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer, in rclone's local configuration file. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
